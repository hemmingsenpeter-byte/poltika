## Ambiguity

D11's single, no-children s 34 slice did not by itself approve excluding
under-18 recipients. The draft selected the ordinary adult category. The
question was whether to retain it or include the separate younger category
before the first slice; it was not whether SLP legally starts at age 18.

## Evidence considered

- `supported-living-payment-social-security-act-20260926`:
  [SSA, version 26 September 2026](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2018/32/en/2026-09-26/),
  s 34(c), permits the s 34 route from age 16, subject to its other requirements.
- Same dated Act, Schedule 4 Part 3 cl 1(a)–(b): paragraph (a) covers single
  beneficiaries under 18 without dependent children; paragraph (b) covers
  other single beneficiaries without dependent children. They are separate
  rate categories, not permission to substitute the adult category.
- Pete's Q25/D44 retains the under-18 exclusion as a separate recorded
  first-slice choice. The age/category provisions were rechecked on
  16 October 2026; no monetary amount is adopted here.

## Options and decision

1. **Selected:** retain the ordinary adult cl 1(b) category, age 18 or over,
   single and without SLP-dependent children throughout the reference week.
2. Source, specify and review the separate under-18 category before the first slice.

For the proposed `[2026-10-05, 2026-10-12)` week, a known under-18 category
or within-week category change is `unsupported / not_modelled`, with no
amount. Unknown or contradictory age/rate-category facts give `needs_review`;
known coverage exclusions take precedence. Require the attested category
for the whole week; do not infer it from another policy's age fact or select
the nearest rate. This does not declare 16–17-year-old grant recipients
legally ineligible for SLP.

## Rationale, limits and reopening

This bounds the first restricted-work-capacity existing-grant slice to one
category. MSD capacity, residence, grant and payment decisions remain asserted
facts. It does not assess medical evidence or expand the s 34 route.

The shared [SPS/SLP weekly-agreement decision](../../sole_parent_support/decisions/DEC-003-weekly-agreement-first-slice.md)
separately governs assessment-basis coverage. Gross/precision DEC-001 remains
open, even at zero income; specification, independent-review, values and
implementation gates remain. Pete may commission the younger category as
a separately sourced and reviewed extension. No rate is approved by this record.
