# DEC-004 — SPS s 30(a) and ordinary-care first slice

**Policy:** Sole Parent Support, existing-grant first slice.
**Date:** 2026-10-16
**Decision owner:** Pete
**Decision date:** 2026-10-16
**Status:** decided for coverage only; the first-slice specification is now `specification_approved` (Pete, 16 October 2026, D47); no value is approved.
**Class:** Assumption — an explicit model coverage restriction, not a statutory eligibility rule.
**Affected rules:** SPS-03, SPS-04 and SPS-06.
**Authority:** [Q25 / D44, recorded on main](https://github.com/hemmingsenpeter-byte/poltika/blob/ed39fe700532b1d22b5a7ae2a3daa44963ce9cf6/docs/decision_log.md).

## Ambiguity

D11's single principal-caregiver existing-grant commission did not itself
record the draft's narrower s 30(a)-only route and exclusion of shared/split
care. The question was whether to retain that boundary or model the other
relationship and care routes before the first slice. This is a coverage
choice, not a determination of a person's legal entitlement.

## Evidence considered

- `sole-parent-support-social-security-act-2026-09-26`:
  [SSA, version 26 September 2026](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2018/32/en/2026-09-26/),
  ss 29–30. Section 30(a) is the single route; paragraphs (b)–(e) provide
  distinct circumstances. Other routes must not be rewritten as ineligibility.
- Same dated Act, ss 31–32 and Schedule 4 Part 2 cl 3: child attribution,
  split care and the specified additional relationship circumstances require
  their own treatment. A principal-caregiver assertion does not resolve them.
- Pete's Q25/D44 expressly retains the s 30(a)-only narrowing, with this
  individual decision record required before specification approval. The
  scoped provisions were rechecked on 16 October 2026; no rate was checked
  or adopted by this follow-up.

## Options and decision

1. **Selected:** retain the existing ordinary s 30(a) route with no shared
   or split care, child-attribution discretion or caregiver selection to resolve.
2. Extend and review the other s 30 routes and care cases before the first slice.

Require the caller's independently classified route and ordinary care facts
throughout the proposed `[2026-10-05, 2026-10-12)` week. A known other route,
shared/split care or a case requiring the module to choose attribution or
caregiver is `unsupported / not_modelled`. Unknown or contradictory route
or care facts give `needs_review`; known coverage exclusions take precedence.
Do not infer these facts from an address, a generic child count or another
policy's child classification. No outside-slice case becomes a numeric zero.

## Rationale, limits and reopening

This keeps the first existing-grant slice bounded without claiming that the
other statutory routes or shared/split-care recipients cannot qualify.
The grant, child, ordinary eligibility and payable-state assertions remain
separate. The shared [DEC-003](DEC-003-weekly-agreement-first-slice.md)
governs income-assessment coverage; neither decision supplies a monetary method.

Gross-up and precision DEC-001/002 remain open. Specification approval,
independent review, values and implementation gates remain unchanged.
Pete may expand the route/care boundary through a separately sourced and
reviewed specification; no automatic extension follows from this decision.
