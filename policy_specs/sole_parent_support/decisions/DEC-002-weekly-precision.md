# DEC-002 — SPS weekly income, deduction and output precision

**Policy:** Sole Parent Support, ordinary weekly-agreement sub-slice.
**Date:** 2026-10-13 (raised / source check).
**Decision owner:** Pete. **Decision date:** none.
**Status:** open for the method; holding position accepted 16 October 2026 (D47); `needs_review`. **Raised by:** GPT/Codex.
**Class:** AI hypothesis.
**Classification:** `MISSING_SOURCE`; any provisional convention is a Tier C choice.

## Ambiguity

The bounded source reading did not establish a complete SPS procedure for
fractional income, intermediate deductions, final statutory/gross amounts
and tie-breaking. Printed monetary amounts with two decimals do not settle
those steps. The absence of a rule in this check is not a finding that no
official procedure exists.

## Evidence considered

- `sole-parent-support-social-security-act-2026-09-26`:
  [Schedule 2 Income Test 1 and Schedule 4 Part 2](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2018/32/en/2026-09-26/)
  supply the statutory rate and withdrawal bands. The scoped provisions
  do not establish the complete fractional/tie procedure examined here.
- `sole-parent-support-social-security-regulations`:
  [r 205 / Schedule 6](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2018/202/en/2026-09-04/)
  determine the income assessment basis, not the complete numeric precision.
- `sole-parent-support-msd-income` and `sole-parent-support-msd-weekly-example`:
  [charging guidance](https://map.workandincome.govt.nz/map/income-support/main-benefits/sole-parent-support/charging-income-01.html)
  and [published example](https://map.workandincome.govt.nz/map/income-support/main-benefits/sole-parent-support/weekly-income-example-01.html)
  corroborate two-band deductions and an integer-income example; neither
  retrieved page supplies a complete fractional-income or tie case.
- `sole-parent-support-income-tax-act-interactions`:
  [RD 11(3)](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-09-04/)
  requires a separately determined main-benefit tax amount. Its operative
  procedure remains missing as recorded in DEC-001.

## Decision

None. No complete-dollar flooring, cent rounding, whole-dollar-only input
restriction or universal exact-output method is selected. The decimal
representation can preserve source observations without deciding a statutory
method. Numeric SPS output remains `needs_review`, even for integer income,
until the complete method and its exact output meaning are reviewed.

## Resolution required

Source the SPS-specific income and deduction procedure, the tax/gross-up
determination, every rounding stage and tie behaviour. Independently validate
threshold fractions and final rounding cases. If primary evidence cannot be
found, Pete may decide an explicitly labelled, bounded Assumption or narrower
slice; that decision must name its limitations and does not become legal truth.
The Q17 income-tax Assumption, JS operational guidance and AS r 19 are not
authority for SPS. No statutory expected value is changed by this record.

## Rationale and confidence

Premature rounding can move withdrawal/zero boundaries and corrupt the
gross component needed by later interactions. Confidence is high that this
bounded source check is incomplete; there is no accepted precision procedure.

## Holding position accepted (Pete, 16 October 2026; decision log D47)

Pete accepted the holding position recorded above: no gross amount is
produced, and the component stays `needs_review`, until a sourced and
independently reviewed method exists. This decides no conversion, gross-up,
precision or rounding method; the method itself remains open with Pete as
owner, and needs a located primary source (the RD 11(3) determination or
equivalent official procedure), not an approval.
