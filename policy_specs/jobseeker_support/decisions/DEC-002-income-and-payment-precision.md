## Ambiguity

The commissioned gross output requires a complete sourced income-preparation,
conversion and precision method; this bounded check has not established it.

## Evidence considered

[Act Schedule 2 Income Test 3(b)](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2018/32/en/2026-09-26/)
states $0.70 reduction for each $1 above $160 weekly. The scoped text does
not specify whole-dollar income preparation, fractional-dollar treatment,
payment rounding, ties or gross-up intermediate precision. Section 24(2)
separately preserves qualifying entitlement during a temporary period in
which income reduces payment to zero; the complete monetary boundary and
administrative determination cannot be inferred from a chosen clamp.

[MSD calculating the benefit rate](https://map.workandincome.govt.nz/map/income-support/main-benefits/jobseeker-support/changes-and-reviews-jobseeker-support/calculating-the-benefit-rate-01.html)
instructs income to be rounded down to the nearest dollar in its discussion
of estimating the benefit rate in DREW. [Its worked examples](https://map.workandincome.govt.nz/map/income-support/main-benefits/jobseeker-support/changes-and-reviews-jobseeker-support/examples-of-income-assessment-01.html)
exclude cents from income before applying the reduction and deduct from net
maxima. That is positive evidence of an operational convention, checked
13 October 2026. Neither live page has an independently dated historical
version. The examples do not establish this single person's gross output.

The unresolved readings are whether the whole-dollar convention applies to
this statutory calculation or only the described estimate, and whether it
acts on the final aggregate chargeable amount or on constituent incomes.
No aggregation-order rule is inferred from couple examples. Final benefit
rounding, intermediate gross conversion and ties remain unverified; a
two-decimal published rate does not establish a universal rounding method.

## Pending decision and effect

**No monetary procedure has been decided.** As provisional evidence-preservation
handling, retain the caller's exact policy-week amount and do not floor it
on intake. This preserves the asserted input for later review; it selects
neither an unrounded calculation nor a floor-first method. JS-08 must not
promote either procedure until independently reviewed and decided. JS-09 additionally
requires DEC-001's gross procedure. All numeric output stays `needs_review`
with no amount while either gate is unresolved. AS/WFF and annual tax
rounding conventions are not transferred.

The smallest next step is source-backed clarification of the income
preparation stage and complete benefit/gross precision, including a
fractional-income example and nil-payment boundary. Pete decides the
interpretation after review; any deliberate model restriction or convention
requires its own written scope/class and cannot become statutory evidence.
