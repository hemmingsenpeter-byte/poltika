# DEC-001 — Jobseeker gross entitlement after income abatement

**Policy:** Jobseeker Support, D11 single 25+ established-grant slice.
**Raised:** 2026-10-13, GPT/Codex. **Decision owner:** Pete.
**Decision date:** none. **Status:** open; `needs_review`.
**Class:** AI hypothesis; discrepancy `MISSING_SOURCE` for the complete
gross-conversion method. A later sourced interpretation is a Rule; an
explicit convention where law is silent must be labelled Assumption.

## Ambiguity and evidence

D11 commissions gross weekly entitlement, not a net-payment component.
The [Act s 17 and Schedule 4 Part 1 cl 1(d)](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2018/32/en/2026-09-26/)
and [2026 Order cls 2–5 / Schedule 1 Part 1](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2026/36/en/2026-03-02B/)
give $372.55 weekly subject to Test 3. [MSD's dated rate row](https://map.workandincome.govt.nz/map/deskfile/main-benefits-rates/jobseeker-support-current.html)
labels that amount net after M tax and separately publishes gross $426.12.
[MSD's worked assessments](https://map.workandincome.govt.nz/map/income-support/main-benefits/jobseeker-support/changes-and-reviews-jobseeker-support/examples-of-income-assessment-01.html)
reduce net maxima. All were checked on 13 October 2026; live MAP material
has no verified immutable historical version.

[Act ss 349–351](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2018/32/en/2026-09-26/)
define the tax-on-benefit mechanism. Section 350(3) uses the applicable
specified tax provision to determine tax on an increased payment which,
after tax, leaves the source deduction payment. Section 351 treats that tax
as benefit received and income. This establishes a relationship, but the
complete dated tax basis, income-dependent conversion, rounding and matching
single-person worked evidence have not been established in this check.

Applying Test 3 directly to $426.12 would choose a different base from
the published net assessments. Treating $372.55 as gross would mislabel
the commissioned output. Applying a constant gross/net ratio would invent
a conversion. None is selected. Annual basic salary/wage income tax is a
different component and cannot stand in for this payment-level procedure.
