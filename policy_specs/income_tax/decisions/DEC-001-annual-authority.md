## Ambiguity

Pete's D11 and the current user instruction keep the 2026–27 annual-rate
authority gate unresolved until enacted legislation is verified. Bill 344-1
remains rank-4 proposed-law context and cannot clear it.

## Evidence considered

New primary-source evidence: [Tax Administration Act 1994, dated 4 September
2026, s 92A(1),(2)](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1994/166/en/2026-09-04/),
read 8 October, permits the Commissioner to assess at basic rates before an
annual taxing Act, expressly preserves that assessment's validity, and
adjusts it if annual rates differ. [Income Tax Act BB 1 and Schedule 1
Part A cl 1](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-09-04/)
remain relevant. The permission names the Commissioner; its existence alone
does not promote a Poltika annual-law output or a proposed Bill to authority.
