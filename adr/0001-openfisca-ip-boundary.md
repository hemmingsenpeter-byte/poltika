# ADR 0001: OpenFisca IP boundary

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-20

## Context

Poltika needs a clear boundary for using external prior art without importing authoritative policy logic from other systems.

## Decision

Poltika will not treat OpenFisca or similar implementations as the primary legal or policy source. External implementations may be used only as a structural reference, never as evidence for statutory interpretation.

## Consequences

- Policy logic must be grounded in primary NZ sources
- Structural patterns may be reviewed, but not copied as policy truth
- Interpretation ambiguity remains an explicit review item
