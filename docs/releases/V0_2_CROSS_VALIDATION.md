## Sources and reasons

**families with family tax credit.** Published: Working for Families statistics datasets (working-for-families-statistics.xlsx), Tax years 2001-2025 (workbook modified 4 September 2026; 2025 marked New) (wff.families_entitled.family_tax_credit).

- synthetic development population, not an estimate for New Zealand
- modelled at full take-up with 2026-27 rates; the published figure is a different period
- the model covers only in-work, non-beneficiary households (assumption A6); beneficiary families also receive family tax credit
- only four household types are represented, and 'three or more' children count as three (A1)
- income, household type and children are drawn independently, so their real correlation is lost

**Accommodation Supplement recipients.** Published: Benefit Fact Sheets: national benefit tables, June 2026, June 2026 quarter (accommodation_supplement.recipients).

- synthetic development population, not an estimate for New Zealand
- modelled at full take-up with 2026-27 rates; the published figure is a different period
- the published count covers all ages and every recipient, beneficiaries included, while the model has only in-work non-beneficiaries (A6)
- homeowners are unsupported in the model (rent or board only)
- AS area shares are assumed equal (A5), so caps and entry points are not New Zealand's mix
