## Constraints on published aggregates

**AI hypothesis — candidate Fact.** These are selected rules in **S9's dated
edition**, not an exhaustive release checklist or evidence of approval:

| Output | Source reading | Locator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counts | Unweighted counts: random rounding to base 3. IRD counts below 6, including zero: suppress first. | 4.1.3; 4.11.4.1, pp 11, 16–17 |
| Survey-weighted counts | Dataset-specific thresholds/bases and additional survey rules apply. | 4.2, pp 11–13 |
| Household totals/means | Suppress below 20 observations; means use rounded counts. | 4.3.3, p 13 |
| Percentages/ratios | Use rounded counts; suppress if constituent counts are suppressed. | 4.5.3–4.5.4, p 14 |
| Suppression | Protect suppressed cells against reconstruction using secondary suppression. | 4.11.2–4.11.3, p 16 |
| Simulated/synthetic output | Suppress below 6 or other applicable suppression; provide creation method and a simulation label. | 4.15, p 18 |
| Code/logs | Subject to checking too; exclude unit records/counts from comments. | 4.9, p 16 |

**AI hypothesis — application to Poltika.** Plan coarse cells and explicit
suppression states, keep check evidence inside the lab, and present only the
released precision. An interactive explorer would need a pre-cleared aggregate
artifact and controlled query scope; approval of a research project does not
authorise arbitrary new cross-tabulations. Repeated scenario comparisons could
expose small subgroups; a difference table needs its own disclosure analysis.
Synthetic records derived from IDI cannot be presumed safe to export. These
are design implications for Stats NZ to assess, not a selected confidentiality
implementation.

**AI hypothesis — candidate Rule.** Section 54(1) requires authorised use,
compliance with access conditions, reasonable protection against identifying
publication, and disclosure of results and methodologies; s 54(3) permits
summary disclosure. This does not require publication of underlying records.
Statutory exceptions in s 54(2)/(4) are not invoked by this proposal. [S7]

**AI hypothesis — candidate Fact.** S9, chapters 5–7, requires checked release
and appropriate disclaimers; reformatting released output without new
microdata need not be rechecked, with caution about added context. Poltika
would still need its own coverage, uncertainty and scenario labels under its
epistemic constitution; an output check is not model validation.
