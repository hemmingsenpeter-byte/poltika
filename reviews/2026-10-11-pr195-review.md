## Verdict

The proposal is a faithful consolidation of decisions already taken. It adds one real constraint (each
policy's income concept is derived through its own dated, source-backed mapping) and keeps every
statutory/methodological choice gated. It makes no statutory claim of its own. I found no contradiction with
an accepted decision and no unsourced number.

## Statutory or methodological claims needing a primary source

None are asserted as law. Checked:
- The proposal names no rate, threshold, date, rounding rule or entitlement and says it "creates no new
  income exclusion". Nothing in it needs a locator.
- It refers to WFF "family scheme income" and AS "chargeable weekly income" only as existing spec terms; both
  are present in the repository specs (`policy_specs/working_for_families/specification.md`;
  `policy_specs/accommodation_supplement/specification.md` AS-07, AS-10). I did not re-derive those specs
  from primary sources here (outside this task).
- Any future mapping (income tax taxable income, each main benefit's income test) is a statutory claim and
  needs dated NZ primary evidence, as the proposal itself says.

## What stays gated, and why

- Combined disposable income (Task 10): D2 has no chosen official definition; source review is pending
  (v0_3 D2, line 58-60). Component coverage and the common period are unspecified.
- Annualisation of AS or WFF weekly/annual amounts: v0_2 D8 requires a stated method (for example weekly x 52
  or number of weeks in the year). None exists, and `convert_amount` refuses accordingly.
- A single income path through WFF and AS: v0_2 D9, an interpretation question for the specifications.
- EMTR (Task 11): needs the above plus a declared perturbation path (D3, EMTR first, but "subject to the
  reviewed income/period path").
- Income tax precision: separate gate (PR #193, DEC-002).
