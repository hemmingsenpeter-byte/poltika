## Independent review

Primary text: SSA 2018 and SSR 2018 as at 4 September 2026 (the current
latest version), and Rates Order SL 2026/36 as made on 2 March 2026.

- **s 18** is headed "General limitation on receiving more than 1 benefit"
  and applies to a person "in his or her own right".
- **SPS** is ss 29–33, which is exactly Subpart 3. Sch 4 Part 2 cl 1 is
  "subject to Income Test 1". Cl 2 is the childcare disregard. Cl 3 is the
  s 30(b)/(e) partner-income attribution. Cl 4 is the dependent-child
  exclusion.
- **SLP** is ss 34–42, which is exactly Subpart 4. Sch 4 Part 3 cl 1:
  categories (a)–(c), (g) and (h) use Income Test 1, and (d)–(e) use Income
  Test 2. Cl 1(d) covers a partner "granted, in the spouse's or partner's own
  right, a main benefit". SSR rr 10–11 form the SLP subpart.
- **Shared provisions:** Sch 3 cl 8(1)(a) and (l) are the benefit and
  tax-credit exclusions. SSR r 205 and Sch 6 list SLP and SPS together.
