## Findings

Both are proposed `CODE_ERROR`, P2, reproduced before fixing.

1. **Impact includes components the caller did not ask about.**
   `src/poltika/scenarios/intake.py:76` asks the resolver for its global
   ranking, then filters the questions without recalculating their scores.
   With G0's WFF eligibility unknown and AS area missing, asking for FTC
   and AS selects WFF eligibility because it also unblocks three unasked
   WFF components. Each question unblocks one requested component, but
   only area varies calculated amounts across answers; area should rank
   first under the declared impact rule.
2. **Numeric questions use rule order instead of catalogue order.**
   `src/poltika/scenarios/intake.py:94` preserves the resolver's rule-sorted
   missing list. With G0's family scheme income and weekly accommodation
   costs missing, it asks for AS costs even though the supplied catalogue
   lists WFF income first. Reversing the catalogue should reverse that
   priority; rule prefixes should not override the caller's catalogue.

## Fixes

- `resolve(impact_components=...)` scopes impact scores to the requested
  components. Defaults retain the previous all-component ranking, and
  results, missing facts and bounds still describe the whole household.
- Intake explicitly orders non-branchable facts by the supplied catalogue.
- Two intake regressions reproduce the findings; resolver checks cover
  invalid component selections and preservation of full household results.
- The plan's attestation wording now matches the PR's documented yes-only
  answer contract, with an unavailable attestation recorded as declined.
